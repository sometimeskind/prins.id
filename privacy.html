Privacy

Last updated 15 September 2026

calendar-sync is a personal application that copies my own Google Calendars onto a server I operate. It has one user: me.

Data it accesses
Google Calendar events, read-only, over Google's CalDAV API, authorised with OAuth 2.0 using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope. Only the calendars I have explicitly listed in its configuration. It never writes to Google.
How it uses them
To keep a copy of my own calendars readable from my own devices. Nothing else.
How it stores them
On hardware I own and operate, for as long as I keep running the application.
Who it shares them with
Nobody. Not sold, not shared with third parties, not sent to any analytics or advertising service, not used to train anything.
